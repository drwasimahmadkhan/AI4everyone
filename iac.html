Core concept
What is Generative AI?

Generative AI creates new content from your instructions.

It can generate text, images, presentations, videos, code, music ideas, lesson plans, business proposals, and more.

✍️

Input

A prompt: your goal, context, and style.

✨

Output

A new draft, design, idea, or visual result.

Simple explanation for all disciplines04

Difference
